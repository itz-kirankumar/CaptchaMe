Presence check

Two ways through, pick either. Both work with a finger, a mouse or a keyboard.

1. Follow the ring

Keep your pointer (or finger) on the moving ring for 7 seconds. We judge the shape of your movement, not speed.

2. Tap the beat

Twelve pulses. Tap along with each flash. Keyboard: press Space. No timing pressure, just tap with the pulse.

Press start, then tap with the pulse

3. Catch the flash

Five flashes at random moments. Click each one before it fades. Fast reactions are normal; instant ones are not human.

Press start, then click each flash

4. Follow and catch

Keep your pointer on the ring. When the ring flashes, click (or press Space) without losing it. Two things at once, like a person.
