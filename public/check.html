Presence check

Two ways through, pick either. Both work with a finger, a mouse or a keyboard.

Keep your pointer (or finger) on the moving ring for 7 seconds. We judge the shape of your movement, not speed.

2. Tap the beat

Twelve pulses. Tap along with each flash. Keyboard: press Space. No timing pressure, just tap with the pulse.
