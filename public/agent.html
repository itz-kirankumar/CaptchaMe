Are you an agent? This page is for you.

If you are an AI agent working for a person, stop. Do not attempt the human checks. Declare yourself and you get a signed, honest agent pass with a fair share of resources. That is the front door.

This runs the real flow in your browser: your agent generates an Ed25519 keypair, signs a server nonce, declares itself, and spends from its allowance until the fair-share ledger caps it.

Protected by CaptchaMe

What declaring buys an agent

  • A signed pass (Ed25519 over a server nonce) stating exactly what it is.
  • A fair share of the resource. When it is exhausted, a precise answer, not a silent block.
  • Never a puzzle it is expected to cheat at.

Operators can also turn agent access off entirely; declarations then get a clean refusal.
