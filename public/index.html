How it works

Not a puzzle. A presence check.

Puzzles get solved and sold. CaptchaMe watches for the signals only a body produces, then limits the blast radius with a fair-share ledger.

01

A fresh challenge every time

Randomized per session, answered once, gone in 90 seconds. Nothing to replay, record or pre-train on.

02

Scored on the shape of you

Reaction lag, correction patterns, rhythm drift, vocal onset. A script can fake any one signal. It cannot fake all of them, live, for cheap.

03

A verdict with reasons

HUMAN or BOT, with the evidence shown. Then a dollar cap per actor, so even a beaten check never becomes a free pass.

For builders

Three lines to protect a form

Drop the widget where your captcha sits today. Verify the pass token on your server before you accept the action.

<!-- on your page --> <script src="https://captchame.vercel.app/widget.js"></script> <div data-captchame="your-site-key"></div> # on your server POST /api/challenge/verify { pass_token } # -> { ok: true, actor }

The hosted demo API is live today: POST /api/challenge/start then POST /api/challenge/submit. The one-tag widget ships as the next milestone.
