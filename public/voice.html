The voice path

The accessible way through the door: no pointer, no timed visual test. Four words appear and are spoken, one at a time. Say each word back when you hear it. We check that a live voice answers, and what it says.

Press start when ready

Needs a microphone. Words are matched on our server by Sarvam's speech recognition; your browser's own recognition is the automatic fallback. Timing is measured from your mic either way.
