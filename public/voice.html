Voice presence

The accessible path through the door: no pointer, no timed visual test. Four words appear and are spoken, one at a time. Say each word back when you hear it. We check that a live voice answers, and what it says.

Press start when ready

Needs a microphone and, for word matching, Chrome or Edge (their built-in speech recognition). Timing is measured from your mic directly, so it works even where speech recognition does not.
