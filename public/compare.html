The asymmetry that broke captchas

Old captchas scale linearly for the attacker: a thousand solves costs a thousand tiny fees, or nothing with a modern agent. CaptchaMe makes each attempt cost live, measured seconds of presence, and the fair-share ledger means scaling up does not dilute the cost. Verification flips who does the work.
